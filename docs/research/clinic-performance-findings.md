# Clinic-performance findings

## Available reporting

The current CVC reporting directory describes:

- an executive control-tower view across open clinics;
- clinic, market, and network comparisons;
- a trailing-13-week performance snapshot;
- customer, sales, appointment, forward-booking, cancellation, and no-show views;
- additional reporting for prescriptions, inventory, memberships, and selected financials.

This confirms that relevant reporting exists. It does not confirm access to underlying rows, stable metric definitions, or authorization to train a model.

## Documented variation

The Atlanta research states that the Alpharetta-area site was growing but underperforming other Atlanta locations despite similar marketing and operational support. The research investigated customer awareness, provider-choice preferences, switching triggers, trust, reviews, appointment availability, wait times, cost, and local discovery contexts.

No numerical performance conclusion from that research has been copied into this repository. The linked results artifact should be reviewed by an authorized analyst before any causal claim or model feature is created.

## Positive developments found

- A production reporting hub exists across operational and business domains.
- Multiple comparison levels are documented.
- Local qualitative research was initiated in response to site variation.
- The existing site-selection plan explicitly proposes learning from historical performance.

## Risks and negative developments found

- At least one site showed weaker client-acquisition interest than its local peers.
- Similar marketing and operational support did not by itself explain the difference.
- The target performance metric for the site-selection model remains unresolved.
- Access to sensitive finance and performance information is role-controlled.

Sources: SRC-001, SRC-002, SRC-007.
