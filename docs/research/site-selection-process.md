# Site-selection process

## Evidence-backed current-state outline

The available research supports this high-level flow:

1. **Define business objective and performance measure.** The existing internal plan requires agreement on a clinic performance metric.
2. **Assemble historical site features and outcomes.** The plan anticipates approximately 30 to 50 historical clinics.
3. **Evaluate geospatial and market context.** Reported inputs include customer reach, drive time, competition, traffic, nearby businesses, population, and demographics.
4. **Compare and rank candidates.** The existing plan proposes predictive modeling, feature importance, weights, and a ranking method.
5. **Apply human and physical diligence.** Interview evidence says final decisions still involve expert judgment and sometimes physical visits.
6. **Move selected sites into opening execution.** Separate artifacts cover the site pipeline, go-to-market activity, system creation, staffing, risk, and operational readiness.
7. **Measure actual performance.** The reporting directory provides network, market, and clinic comparison views.
8. **Feed outcomes back into selection.** The existing roadmap proposes expanding features, scenario simulation, and time-series modeling.

## Important distinction

Site selection and clinic opening are related but different workflows. This MVP initially stops at a reviewable candidate comparison. It does not automate lease diligence, staffing approval, technical provisioning, go-to-market activation, or opening readiness.

## Missing process evidence

- Formal decision gates and decision owners
- Current weighting logic
- Real-estate cost and lease constraints
- Mandatory physical-site criteria
- Staffing feasibility rules
- Required approval and audit record
- Post-opening outcome window used to label success
