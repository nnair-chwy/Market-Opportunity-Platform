## Source-use rules

- Verify current dashboards, owners, plans, and location counts before using them in a decision.
- Do not copy raw customer data or restricted dashboard exports into this repository.
- Meeting notes may support discovery questions but do not establish production requirements by themselves.
- Historical launch documents are context, not proof of the current process.
