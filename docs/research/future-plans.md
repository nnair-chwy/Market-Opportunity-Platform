# Future plans

## Confirmed site-selection roadmap

The internal site-selection plan describes three future directions:

1. Add internal and external market, demographic, competitor, real-estate, customer, and engagement attributes.
2. Add scenario simulation for overlap, trade areas, competitive density, and demographic assumptions.
3. Add weekly or monthly performance modeling to forecast footfall, visits, and demand.

## Reported expansion activity

Meeting notes state that Chewy is actively evaluating new clinic locations. The Site Pipeline is the referenced source for current opening dates, but access has not been confirmed.

## Product implication

The first implementation should preserve extension points for additional features, simulations, and time series without claiming to deliver them. The near-term decision remains whether this repo complements or duplicates the existing internal plan.

## Current-location reference

Use the live [official Chewy Vet Care locations page](https://www.chewy.com/vet-care/) as the public reference. Do not maintain a manually copied location list as a source of truth.
