# Clinic-opening process

The collected sources show several parts of opening execution, but not one complete end-to-end playbook.

## Documented components

### Pipeline and dates

The site-experience playbook points to a Smartsheet Site Pipeline for current opening dates. The pipeline itself has not been accessed.

### Go-to-market

The playbook defines pre-launch and launch activity aimed at customers within a 30-minute drive-time area. Booking rate is the primary documented KPI and click-through rate is secondary.

### System creation

The Kyrios runbook describes a Jira-triggered process that creates a clinic organization, performs required status transitions, affiliates the site, corrects address formatting, and verifies the final record. This is one technical workstream, not the whole launch.

### Staffing and configuration

Interview notes report that new-site staffing uses demand formulas, state roles vary, doctors are recruited before nurses, and each Rhapsody location requires substantial manual configuration. These points require owner validation.

### Launch risk

The historical first-opening risk plan considered payment, account authentication, cash, incentives, card-present transactions, monitoring checkpoints, and rollback triggers. It is useful as a pattern, but may not represent the current opening process.

## Missing end-to-end evidence

- Real-estate approval and lease gates
- Construction and facilities readiness
- Regulatory and veterinary licensing
- Hiring readiness thresholds
- Full system and identity provisioning checklist
- Data and reporting readiness
- Marketing activation criteria
- Opening-day command structure
- Post-opening review cadence

Sources: SRC-004, SRC-005, SRC-006, SRC-008, SRC-012.
