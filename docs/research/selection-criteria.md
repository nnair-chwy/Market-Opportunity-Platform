# Selection criteria

This is an evidence inventory, not an approved scoring model.

| Criterion family | Candidate measures | Evidence status | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer demand | Customer count or density within approved trade area; digital engagement; market penetration | Reported or roadmap | SRC-001, SRC-003 |
| Accessibility | Drive-time coverage; overlap with existing clinics; trade-area definition | Reported or roadmap | SRC-001, SRC-003 |
| Competition | Existing veterinary clinics; competitor count or density | Reported | SRC-003 |
| Market context | Population density; demographic signals; nearby businesses | Reported | SRC-003 |
| Movement | Foot-traffic measure or approved proxy | Reported | SRC-003 |
| Real estate | Site and market attributes; cost and lease constraints | Partial | SRC-001 |
| Staffing | Veterinarian demand and role feasibility | Reported, not defined | SRC-008 |
| Local demand quality | Awareness, trust, switching triggers, reviews, availability expectations, cost perceptions | Confirmed qualitative research themes | SRC-007 |
| Operational outcome | Revenue, traffic, registered patients, appointments, customers, sales, forward booking, cancellation and no-show | Confirmed as examples or dashboard domains | SRC-001, SRC-002 |

## Rules for scoring

- No criterion receives a production weight until its definition, direction, range, source, freshness, and owner are approved.
- Do not collapse qualitative findings into numeric scores without an explicit rubric.
- Distinguish a screening threshold from a preference weight.
- Show missingness and data quality separately from site desirability.
- Preserve raw metrics, normalized metrics, weights, and final contributions.
- Run sensitivity analysis so reviewers can see whether rankings depend on fragile weight choices.
